## Initial Findings

Based on the analysis of the sales dataset, the following findings were identified:

- **Highest Monthly Revenue:** August recorded the highest monthly revenue.
- **Highest Monthly Profit:** February recorded the highest monthly profit.
- **Top Revenue and Profit Category:** Electronics generated the highest revenue and profit, making it the strongest category in terms of financial contribution.
- **Leading Sales Region:** Nairobi recorded the highest product sales, followed by Rift Valley.
- **Highest Unit Sales Across Regions:** Accessories recorded the highest sales volume across regions, even though Electronics generated more revenue and profit. This highlights the difference between the quantity of products sold and their financial contribution.
